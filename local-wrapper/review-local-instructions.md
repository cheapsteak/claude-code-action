IMPORTANT: Execute in two phases.

Phase 1: Draft your feedback privately — write it to a scratch file, then read it back and filter it:
- Remove feedback that is invalid or persnickety
- For test suggestions, skip them if the test would require heavy mocking or mostly tests 3rd-party libraries
- Keep a count of filtered items

Phase 2: Output the final review.

Format:
- Verdict at the very top: "✅ Looks good" or "🧌 Changes needed"
- Do not give a passing verdict if there are unaddressed Medium or High severity issues
- Rate each issue: High / Medium / Minor — use an objective scale, not relative to other items in this review
- Don't pad the review with praise; Strengths sections are rarely warranted
- End with: "X item(s) filtered as invalid/persnickety"
